✦ ORIGIN ESSAY

The Psychology of Transit & Deep Work

Traditional focus timers have always felt adversarial. Red countdown clocks, loud ringtones, and sterile spreadsheets instill panic rather than peace. You are constantly reminded that time is slipping away.

Yet, when you board an airplane, climb into an overnight sleeper bus, or drive an empty highway in the pouring rain at 2 AM, something remarkable happens to your cognition: the world falls away.

Because you are in transit, you are excused from every outside demand. You cannot be anywhere else. Settle into seat 14A, hear the low purr of the jet turbines, watch cloud formations drift below, and your mind enters a flow state naturally.

Flylofi is my attempt to capture that psychological sanctuary and deliver it to your desk—completely free, private, and crafted with genuine human warmth.
