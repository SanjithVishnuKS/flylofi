OFFICIAL ATELIER COVENANT · EFFECTIVE 2026

Flylofi Focus Flight was built upon a fundamental architectural promise: deep work requires peace of mind. You should never be forced to sacrifice your personal location, screen activity, or device telemetry to enter a focused flow state.

1. Mathematical Great-Circle Coordinates (Zero GPS)

Unlike competing focus flight apps that query Apple/Android device location APIs or require invasive Screen Time permissions, Flylofi calculates all avionics radar projections mathematically:

  • No Location Permissions: Flylofi never requests or accesses your device GPS, Wi-Fi tri-lateration, or IP geofencing.
  • Spherical Trigonometry: Reachable destinations, radar ranges, and flight distances are computed client-side using Great-Circle distance formulas from your chosen departure hub.

2. 100% Client-Side Local Storage

All your focus statistics, completed journey logs, stamped immigration visas, and alliance tier status are saved exclusively in your browser or device's localStorage. No telemetry is sent to any external server.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

Flylofi is completely free of Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, ad networks, tracking SDKs, or background analytics. When you disconnect from the internet, the app continues to operate seamlessly offline.

4. Universal Sovereign Freedom

Available as a standalone Windows executable (Flylofi.exe), Linux archive (flylofi-linux.tar.gz), signed Android package (focus-flight.apk with zero dangerous permissions), and progressive web app (PWA).
