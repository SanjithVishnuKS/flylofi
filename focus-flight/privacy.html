FlyLofi Focus Flight

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

FlyLofi Focus Flight ("FlyLofi", "we", "our") is a focus and productivity application designed to provide relaxing aviation-themed audio and visual sessions. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this policy.

2. Data Collection and Usage

FlyLofi is designed with a strict privacy-first principle:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifying information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or precise GPS locations.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking trackers, or telemetry software.
  • Local Processing: All timer configurations, flight routes, audio playback, and custom user-inserted media files are processed locally on your device.

3. Permissions

When running on Android, FlyLofi may request only standard system permissions necessary for core app functionality:

  • Storage / Media Access: Only accessed if you choose to import custom local music or video files via the "+ Audio" and "+ Video" buttons into your focus session.
  • Network Access: Used exclusively to stream media from your configured local network server or edge worker when online. FlyLofi works seamlessly offline without requiring internet.

4. Children's Privacy

FlyLofi does not target children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@flylofi.org.
